          Rule checks
            Batting order · Rule 12

            Every player at the game is in the batting order, and the order stays the same all game. The only exception is a player removed for injury or illness.

            Infield is P, C, 1B, 2B, SS, 3B. The catcher counts as infield (Rule 15). No player sits a second inning until every player has sat once.

            Rule 9

            Field size

            Standard Cal Ripken bases, 60 ft apart. The 20 ft fair arc is halfway between home and the rubber, so the rubber is about 40 ft from home.

            Rules 17, 18

            Players & helmets

            Only registered players bat or play the field. Every batter and base runner wears a batting helmet.

            Rule 21

            Uniforms

            Shirts tucked in at all times. Hats worn properly.

            Rule 22

            No tobacco or alcohol

            Not on the field, in the dugout, or in the stands. The umpire decides enforcement, up to removing people or forfeiting the game.

            Rule 23

            Behavior

            Head coaches are responsible for their fans, players and coaches. See the Coaches and Parents Codes of Conduct on the league website.

            Rules 6, 24

            Ages & fall season

            Ages are on the league website. In the fall, all teams follow the Spring NL rules, so the “NL Only” rules (10d and 15a) apply.
